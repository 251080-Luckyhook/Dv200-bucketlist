Reaching the summit at 4:12am

📍 Mount Fuji, Japan   |   Adventure    Posted by Marco Le Roux
Adventure Hiking Sunrise

We started the climb at 11pm so we'd reach the summit before sunrise. Somewhere around the eighth station the altitude started to argue with me, but the line of headlamps snaking up the mountain in the dark kept everyone moving. When the sky finally broke gold over the clouds, nobody spoke for a full minute. Worth every one of the four hours of switchbacks.
